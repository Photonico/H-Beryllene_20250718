q is given in the reciprocal basis of POSCAR (`phonopy.yaml`); `phonopy_disp.yaml` was made with `--pa auto`, whose basis is rotated ([[−1, 1], [−1, 0]]), so the same point reads (0, 0.4) there.

1. **Functional mismatch (error).** The geometries (2.2 relaxations → 6.0_dielectric_selection) are PBE without D3 and SIGMA 0.05, but the force runs use IVDW = 12 and SIGMA 0.1. The structures are therefore not at equilibrium for the force field used (residual forces 0.014–0.063 eV/Å). Phonons must use the relaxation settings.
2. **Band path misses inequivalent directions (error in the reported stability).** All band.conf files use the hexagonal Γ–K–M–Γ path, but the cells are C2/m, P1 and P-1. For 2H-β the soft mode at the commensurate q = (0.4, 0.6) (direction b1 − b2) is off the path, so the plotted dispersion showed only −0.013 THz at Γ. It survives removal of the residual forces and appears only with the denser 13×13 k mesh (not with 5×5), i.e. it looks electronic (Fermi-surface driven), not numerical.
3. **1H-β imaginary pocket is an interpolation artefact.** All commensurate q are stable; the −0.6 THz (path) / −1.4 THz (fine grid) pocket lies at small q on the flexural (ZA) branch and does not change with force-constant symmetrisation or residual-force removal.

## Results of the fixes (2026-09-28)

- Frozen phonon, 2H-β soft mode (PBE, no D3, SIGMA 0.05): E0 relative to A0 is +0.58 meV (0.048 Å) and +12.45 meV (0.097 Å) per
  20-atom cell. The undistorted structure does not lower its energy along the mode, but the rise is strongly anharmonic
  (ratio 21 instead of 4): a fit E = aA² + bA⁴ gives a quadratic term close to zero (slightly negative), i.e. any double well is
  ~0.02 meV deep. The mode is nearly zero-frequency in the harmonic sense and needs careful convergence in any e-ph calculation.
- 2H-α PBE rerun (3.7_phonon_dispersion_with_hydrogen_pbe/a-Beryllene_hh, 6/6 done): residual force 4.6e-4 eV/Å, no imaginary
  frequency on the commensurate grid or a 60×60 grid; frequencies agree with the old PBE+D3 run within 0.05 THz at Γ.
- 1H-β PBE rerun: 17/18 displacements done; disp-005 hit the 12 h walltime and was resubmitted (job 43538, 16 h).
- 2H-β PBE rerun: 12 displacements submitted as array 43539[] (24 cores, 24 h each).

## Results of the fixes (2026-10-01)

- 2H-β PBE rerun complete (12/12, array 43597[]): residual force 6.9e-3 eV/Å; **−1.649 THz at the commensurate
  q = (0.4, 0.6)**, same eigenvector as the old PBE+D3 run (overlap 1.000, −0.646 THz there), 99% Be weight; imaginary pocket
  1.1% of the BZ around ±(0.4, −0.4). D3 stiffens the mode; the next three modes at this q change by < 0.07 THz.
- Frozen phonon with A05 (0.024 Å) and SIGMA 0.02 / 0.10 series (43606–43614): fit E = αQ² + βQ⁴ gives a harmonic 1.02i THz
  (SIGMA 0.05) / 1.13i THz (0.10) and a double well of only 0.021 / 0.032 meV per 20-atom cell; SIGMA 0.02 (16 × 97 k) is not
  converged (fit residual 0.16 meV). Undistorted 2H-β is a saddle point, but the well is ~100× below the zero-point energy of
  the mode: no static distortion expected; strongly anharmonic (harmonic e-ph calculations will show the imaginary pocket).
- Pristine PBE phonons (3.8_phonon_dispersion_pbe): α stable everywhere, ST stable, β (P-1 geometry) stable at commensurate q
  with a ZA artefact near Γ but residual force 0.045 eV/Å. The β geometry itself is a k-mesh artefact: relaxed at 27 × 27 k it
  becomes P-3m1 (2.0_geometry_optimization/b-Beryllene_p3m1).
- Symmetry tolerance: α and 2H-α are ~1e-4 Å off hexagonal; phonopy's default 1e-5 finds Cmmm / C2/m and the interpolation
  breaks C6 (artificial −1.29 THz pocket in α). Use 1e-3 (band.yaml regenerated with --tolerance 1e-3).
- Report: 9.0_topology/docs/topo_report_20261001.md, Section 3.
